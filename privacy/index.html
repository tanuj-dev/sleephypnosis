Sleep Hypnosis

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Sleep Hypnosis ("the app") is made by Tanuj Prajapati. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal information. Everything you do in it stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Thoughts you type or tap before a session. They are used only to show them during that session. They are never uploaded or backed up by the app.
  • Your settings, such as session length, soundscape and volume.
  • Your sleep history: the nights you used the app and how long each session lasted.

All sound is generated on your phone while it plays. The app does not stream or download audio.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone or health data.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription or the lifetime unlock, payment is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. To confirm your purchase, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous purchase identifier and your purchase history from Apple. It does not receive your name or email address. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for use by or near infants.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
