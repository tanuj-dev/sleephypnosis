Common questions

Does it need an internet connection? No. Every sound is generated on your phone, so it works in airplane mode.

Will it keep playing when my screen is locked? Yes. Leave the app running in the background and lock your phone as usual.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone? Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription? Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Sleep Hypnosis. Cancel at least a day before it renews.

Is my information private? Yes. What you type stays on your phone and is never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

Sleep Hypnosis is a relaxation aid. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
